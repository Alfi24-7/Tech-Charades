How to Play

Basic Rules

  • Each team has 4 students.
  • One member acts per turn — they are the Actor.
  • Actor picks a chit from the bowl without showing anyone.
  • Use only gestures, body language, and expressions.
  • NO speaking, mouthing, pointing at objects, or writing in the air.
  • Team has a set time limit to guess the correct term.
  • 1 point per correct guess. No point if time runs out.

During the Game

  • Only the Actor may pick the chit and perform.
  • Guessing members must speak clearly for the scorekeeper.
  • Once time is up, the turn ends immediately — no exceptions.
  • Be respectful to other teams and enjoy the experience.
  • Tie-breaker round if two teams share equal scores at the end.
  • Scorekeeper and coordinator's decision is final.
